## Accounts and projects

- An **account** is your organisation.
- A **project** is a workspace inside it. Each project has its own data connection (one BigQuery project), its own code, dashboards, documents, pipelines and notebooks.

If you need to work with a different warehouse, create another project rather than adding a second connection to the same one.

## The workspace switcher

The button at the top of the sidebar shows where you are: your account above, the project below. Click it to:

- **Search** your projects by typing,
- **Switch** to any project you have access to, in any account (use the arrow keys and Enter, or click),
- **Create a new project** (account admins only).

Everything on the page follows the project you picked, and the platform remembers it for next time.

## The sidebar

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| (top) | **Home**: overview of the project. |
| **Data** | Pipelines, Build, Explore, Notebooks: the data tools. |
| **Share** | Dashboards, and Knowledge (your team's documents). |
| **Automate** | **Agent** (ask questions about your data) and **CLI** (the command console), both for editors and admins. |
| **Settings** (bottom) | Activity, project settings, account settings, your profile and access tokens, the light/dark theme, and sign out. |

Above every page title, a small path shows where you are, like `your-account › your-project › build`.

Use the menu button at the top of the sidebar to collapse it to icons.

:::note
The platform keeps your place. If you leave the Agent to check a dashboard and come back, your conversation and any unsent message are still there. This works for the last few pages you visited; older ones start fresh.
:::

## Light and dark mode

Open **Settings** and choose **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (the app starts on Light). Embedded tools such as Explore follow your choice. Pages from other services (for example an external dashboard) keep their own look.
