---
title: Signing in
description: How to sign in, and what to do if you can't.
---

Access to the platform is **by invitation**. Nobody can register themselves: your Reflective Data account manager adds you, and you'll use the same email address to sign in.

## Two ways to sign in

- **Google.** Choose *Continue with Google* and pick the Google account that uses the email address you were invited with.
- **Email code.** Enter your email address, press the button, and we email you a short one-time code. Enter it to sign in. Codes expire after a few minutes.

You stay signed in on that browser until you sign out. To sign out, open **Settings** in the sidebar and choose **Sign out**.

## If it doesn't work

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| "No Reflective Data account exists for that Google address" | The Google account you picked isn't the email you were invited with. Try another Google account, or use the email-code option. |
| "That code is invalid or expired" | Request a new code and use the newest email. |
| You never receive the code | Check spam. If it still doesn't arrive, ask your account manager to confirm the email address they invited. |
| You can sign in but see no project | You haven't been given access to a project yet. Ask an account admin. |

See also [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting/).
