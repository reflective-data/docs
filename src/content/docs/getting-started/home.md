Home is the first page you see. It gives you the state of the project and the quickest ways in.

## What's on it

- **Ask box** (editors and admins, once the [agent is set up](/agent/setup/)). Type a question and press Enter; the agent answers on its own page. A few suggestions sit underneath.
- **Setup checklist.** Shown only while something is missing: connect BigQuery, link your dbt repository, run dbt once, turn on Explore, set up the agent. Each line links to where you fix it. It disappears when everything is done.
- **Status tiles.** Pipelines (how many, and whether any are failing), your last dbt run, how many dashboards you have, and how many documents. Click a tile to open that area.
- **Recent dashboards.** The ones you opened last, then the newest.
- **Quick actions.** Shortcuts to common jobs, based on your role.
- **From Knowledge** and **Your recent chats.** The documents updated last, and your latest conversations with the agent.

Viewers see everything they have access to; actions they can't perform aren't shown.
