**Dashboards** lists every dashboard for the project in one place:

- **Explore** dashboards, built in [Explore](/explore/overview/). You see the ones your role lets you see.
- **External** dashboards: reports from other tools such as Looker Studio, embedded here so people don't have to look elsewhere. Only **admins** add, edit or remove these.

Click one to open it inside the platform. **Sort** the list by *last opened* (your own history), *recently created*, or *A to Z*; your choice is remembered.

## Adding an external dashboard (admins)

Press **+ Add dashboard** and give it a **title** and an **embed link** (https only).

For **Looker Studio**: open the report, choose **File → Embed report**, turn on **Enable embedding**, and paste the link that starts with `https://lookerstudio.google.com/embed/`. Anyone with access to the project can open it, so make sure the report is OK to share that widely.

Optionally set a **minimum** and **maximum width** (in pixels). A maximum keeps a dashboard centred instead of stretching on wide screens; a minimum stops it squeezing on narrow ones (the page then scrolls sideways).

:::note
An external dashboard has its own look and sign-in rules; the platform can't change its theme or track what you do inside it.
:::
