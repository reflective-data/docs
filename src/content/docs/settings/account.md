## Account settings

Open **Settings → Account settings** (account admins).

- **Account name**: rename your account.
- **Projects**: create more projects. Each is a separate workspace with its own connection, code, dashboards, documents and pipelines.
- **Team**: see who has access and change their **role** (admin, editor or viewer) for the account or for individual projects. Adding and removing people is done by your Reflective Data account manager; ask them and they'll invite your colleagues.

## Profile

Open **Settings → Profile** to change your display name. Your email address is your identity and can't be changed there.

## Theme and sign out

The same **Settings** menu has the **Dark / Light / System** switch and **Sign out**.
